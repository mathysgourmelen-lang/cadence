# Privacy policy

_Last updated: November 14, 2026_

Cadence is a desktop app that runs entirely on your Mac. It has no server, no user accounts and no analytics or tracking.

## What Cadence accesses

When you connect an account, Cadence uses Google's and Meta's official sign-in to get permission to:

- **YouTube** (Google): upload videos and set their thumbnails, read your channel's basic details and video statistics, and read YouTube Analytics reports for your channel.
- **Instagram** (Meta): publish reels, list your posts and read their insights (views, reach, watch time and similar).

Cadence uses this access only to do what you ask in the app: post the videos you schedule and show your own statistics.

## Where your data is stored

- Sign-in tokens are stored on your Mac, encrypted with the macOS Keychain.
- Your scheduled posts, settings, copies of your videos and cached statistics are stored in Cadence's folder on your Mac.
- Nothing is sent to the developer of Cadence or to any third party. Cadence only talks directly to Google's and Meta's APIs (and their video hosts) on your behalf.

## Sharing

Cadence does not sell, share or transfer your data. Google user data is used only to provide Cadence's features to you, in line with the [Google API Services User Data Policy](https://developers.google.com/terms/api-services-user-data-policy), including its Limited Use requirements.

## Removing your data

- In Cadence, open **Accounts** and click **Remove** to delete an account's saved sign-in.
- You can also revoke access at any time: Google at [myaccount.google.com/permissions](https://myaccount.google.com/permissions), Meta at Facebook **Settings → Business integrations**.
- Deleting the app and its folder (`~/Library/Application Support/Cadence`) removes everything else.
